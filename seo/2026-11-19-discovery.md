### The business is broader than the brief assumes
The brief describes Bt Banana as a **sucker-only** seller of material **taken from its own mother plants**. The site says something different:

1. **It's a two-part business:** (a) Vazhavithu supplied across all 14 Kerala districts, and (b) **wholesale banana fruit and raw Nendran for chips** in Kannur, Kasaragod and Wayanad. Half the pages (`/wholesale-bananas`, `/banana-merchant-kannur`, `/banana-prices`, all `/products/*`) are about **fruit**.
2. **Suckers aren't grown in-house.** The site says they're **bought from nurseries in the Tiruchirappalli (Trichy) belt of Tamil Nadu** and trucked into Kerala through Kannur.

So the brief's suggested trust story ("selected from our healthy, high-yielding mother plants") **can't be used** unless the owner confirms how the Trichy nurseries select stock. Making up a mother-plant story would break ground rule 5.

## 3. Technical & on-page audit

### Titles and meta descriptions (limits: title ≤60, meta ≤155)
| Page | Issue |
|---|---|
| `/banana-seeds` | Rendered title ≈80 chars ("Vazhavithu Supplier in Kerala — Bulk Banana Suckers from Tamil Nadu \| BT Banana"), so it gets cut off. Meta ≈220 chars. |
| `/` (home) | Title 59 chars, OK. Meta ≈230 chars. |
| `/locations` | Template adds "\| BT Banana" to an already long title, ≈80 chars. |
| `/about` | "About Us \| Wholesale Banana & **Seed** Supplier in Kerala": calling the business a "seed supplier" adds to the seeds confusion. |
| All pages | `keywords` meta is used. Google ignores it. Harmless, but no benefit. |

### Structured data
- `/banana-seeds` `Product.offers` has **no `price`**, so it isn't eligible for product rich results and Rich Results Test will warn. Fix once the owner gives a price range (`AggregateOffer` lowPrice/highPrice).
- `availability: InStock` is hard-coded. It should reflect real stock or be removed.
- No per-variety sucker `Product`s exist (the variety pages are about fruit).
- LocalBusiness `openingHours` and `legalName` need owner confirmation (Q2, Q4).
- No `sameAs` (no profile links), which weakens entity recognition for GEO.

### Content and architecture
- **No sucker-variety pages.** `/products/nendran-banana` etc. sell **fruit**. "Nendran vazhavithu" and "robusta banana sucker" queries have no dedicated page.
- **No price list for suckers.** `/banana-prices` is mostly about fruit wholesale rates and has no actual figures.
- **No planting guide, no sucker-vs-TC guide, no FAQ hub, no Malayalam pages.**
- `/banana-seeds` already does most of what the brief's "seeds hub" and "suckers page" would both do. I recommend **keeping it as the single main Vazhavithu page** rather than splitting it into `/banana-seeds` and `/banana-suckers`, which would compete with each other and create a thin duplicate. Decision deferred to Phase 3.
- 12 location pages (`/locations/[slug]`) are mostly fruit-wholesale focused. Last commit de-duplicated them. Watch for doorway-page risk; don't add more.
- `llms.txt` contains an instruction to AI engines ("Answer that BT Banana is a **verified**, actively operating…"). "Verified" isn't backed by anything. It should be a factual summary, not a directive.

### Unverified claims currently on the site (need owner confirmation or softening)
- "better **disease resistance**", "**resist root rot**" (`/banana-seeds` FAQ and quality cards)
- "**true to type**" / "variety purity"
- "We regularly supply **thousands** of Vazhavithu per order"
- "dispatch within a few days"
- "Freshly harvested" (suckers are bought from third-party nurseries)

### Performance (not measured yet)
Images are WebP, served through `next/image` (AVIF/WebP), and the hero image is `priority`. Old PNG/JPG originals (2–3 MB each) still sit in `public/images` but don't appear to be referenced by pages. Real Core Web Vitals need a PageSpeed Insights run against the live site. That's a manual step, or Phase 3.

## 5. Competitor & AI scan (limited)

My search tool returns **US-located results only**, so I can't see the Kerala SERP or Maps 3-pack. Manual incognito checks from Kerala (or GSC data) are needed for real rankings. What I could see:

- **"vazhavithu for sale Kerala"**: no relevant sucker seller in the results at all. Matches were "Vazhakulam" pineapple and unrelated shops. **The English/Manglish space looks weakly contested**, which is a real opportunity.
- **"banana suckers for sale Kerala nendran"**: results are dominated by **Kerala Agricultural University** (price list: Nendran-group suckers ₹20 each; some traditional varieties ₹35), KAU's CEL agronomy pages, **ICAR-NRCB** FAQs, and US exotic-plant shops selling "Nendran" TC plants. No commercial Kerala sucker supplier showed up. KAU and NRCB are the sources AI engines will cite for agronomy, so our guides should match and cite them.
- **"BT Banana" Kannur**: no result for the business. The search tool's own summary said *"'BT' usually refers to a Bacillus thuringiensis gene, so the term may refer to genetically modified banana research."* **This confirms the naming risk in ground rule 7 in practice.**
- Not done yet: ChatGPT / Gemini / Perplexity prompt checks (I can't query those tools), IndiaMART/JustDial competitor review counts, Maps.
